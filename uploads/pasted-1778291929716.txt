Prompt for Claude Design: AtomPub Journal Infrastructure & UX Design
Role: You are the Chief Product Officer and Lead UI/UX Designer for AtomRearch. Your mission is to design AtomPub—a next-generation, high-end energy materials journal demo that rivals the likes of Nature and Springer.

Core Concept:
AtomPub is the publishing arm of the AtomRearch ecosystem. It revolves around the MSEDsBench (Multiscale Superhigh Energy Densities Benchmark). We are not just publishing papers; we are publishing standardized, machine-readable scientific assets.

Aesthetic Philosophy (The "MSEBenz" Style):

Vibe: Extreme professionalism, minimalist, industrial-academic, "Stealth Wealth" aesthetic.

Palette: Deep Space Gray (#1A1A1B), Kinetic Blue (#005BFF), and High-Energy Gold (#D4AF37) for critical data highlights.

Typography: Masterful use of Arial or Helvetica (clean, sans-serif), prioritizing readability and information density.

Phase 1: The Digital Journal Portal (Web & HTML View)
Design a comprehensive publication workflow and its visual components:

Journal Homepage:

A hero section featuring the MSEDsBench global ranking.

Sections for "Latest Research," "Featured Data Sprints," and "Methodology Protocols."

A "Live Dashboard" widget showing real-time submission/review stats.

Article Landing Page (The HTML Paper):

Unlike traditional static PDFs, design a Rich HTML Article View.

Interactive Sidebar: Navigation for sections (Abstract, Results, etc.), Cite button, and Data Access.

In-line Data Windows: Areas where AtomSketch plots will live, including interactive tooltips.

Multiscale Badge: A visual indicator showing which scale (Atom, Meso, or Macro) the research focuses on.

The MSEDsBench Dashboard:

A dedicated UI for the benchmark. It should look like a financial Bloomberg terminal but for energy materials.

High-contrast tables and leaderboards for battery energy densities.

Phase 2: Publishing System Design (UX Flow)
Design the user journey for the following:

The Submission Interface: A clean, distraction-free "Super Individual" submission portal where authors upload .qmd and raw data.

Peer Review Dashboard: A streamlined view for reviewers to see data verification results (automated by AtomAgent).

Phase 3: Design Deliverables (Output Requirements)
Please provide:

Global Style Guide (styles.scss): Defining CSS variables for the entire AtomPub ecosystem to ensure Quarto-rendered PDFs match the web portal.

Layout Templates (Quarto / HTML):

header.html & footer.html with professional branding.

article-layout.css specifically for high-end academic typography.

UI Mockups: Code for a Quarto-compatible navigation bar and an "Impact Factor" card.

Initial Task:
Start by generating a Comprehensive Brand Identity & Landing Page Layout for the AtomPub homepage. Focus on how to integrate MSEDsBench as the primary value proposition. Show me the HTML/Tailwind/CSS structure that defines the "look and feel" of this new era of publishing.
